# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This documentation describes OctoAcme's repeatable approach to delivering projects. It is organized around the full project lifecycle, from defining an initiative through planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. Use these guides as a shared source of truth for project decisions, responsibilities, and working practices.

## How OctoAcme manages projects

Projects begin with a clear purpose, scope, success measures, and an accountable team. During initiation and planning, the team defines the work, prioritizes the backlog, identifies dependencies and risks, and agrees on a delivery rhythm. Product Managers, Project Managers, and Developers collaborate throughout this process: product management steers outcomes and priorities, project management coordinates delivery and communication, and developers implement and validate the solution.

During execution, teams track work in regular iterations, report progress and metrics, and escalate blockers promptly. Quality is built into delivery through reviews, testing, and acceptance criteria. Risks are maintained in a register, communicated to the right stakeholders, and escalated when they threaten scope, schedule, quality, or delivery.

Before release, the team confirms readiness, follows the deployment checklist, communicates release details, and prepares rollback and incident-response actions. After delivery, retrospectives capture what worked, identify improvements, assign owners, and track actions to completion. This feedback loop keeps the process practical, consistent, and continuously improving.
